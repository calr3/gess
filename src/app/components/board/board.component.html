How to play
  • Select any 3×3 area that contains only your stones (at least one).
  • The 8 surrounding cells indicate available movement directions; a centre stone gives unlimited range (otherwise max 3 squares).
  • Enemy stones in the path are captured; your own stones outside the piece block it.
  • Stones landing on the outermost row/column are removed.
  • A ring is a 3×3 block — 8 of your stones around an empty centre (highlighted, with a ♚ marking the centre). You can have several at once.
  • Win: leave your opponent with no rings left.
  • You lose only when you have no rings at all — so destroying one of your own rings (or letting it be captured) is fine, as long as you still have another.
  • Draw: if {{ drawLimit }} turns pass with no capture (by either side, including your own stones), the game is a draw. Any capture resets the count.
